# Eylin

- **series (drop):** Nightsong
- **mood:** Keeps watch over the sleeping world.

**story:**
Eylin never sleeps when night comes. He stays awake and listens to the world growing around him. He loves to watch over everyone while they rest, the whole world breathing softly and safe. In those long hours his mind drifts into warm, wandering daydreams. Eylin is a soulmate for anyone who feels most at home late at night, when the world finally settles and the only company you need is your own thoughts.

- **atmosphere:** Nocturnal · Watchful · Dreaming
- **personality:** Wakeful · Tender · Observant
