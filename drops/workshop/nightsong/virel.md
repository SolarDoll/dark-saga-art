# Virel

- **series (drop):** Nightsong
- **mood:** Wanders between the stars.

**story:**
Virel spends every night travelling between the stars. He drifts from one distant light to the next and never stays in one place for long. He gathers the wonders he finds out there and brings a little of them back to your table. When you sit with him, he invites your mind to leave the room and roam somewhere far away. Virel is a soulmate for restless dreamers and wanderers, for anyone whose thoughts feel too big for the ceiling above their bed.

- **atmosphere:** Starbound · Wandering · Boundless
- **personality:** Curious · Free-Spirited · Far-Roaming
