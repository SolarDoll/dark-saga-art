# Wynn

- **series (drop):** Nightsong
- **mood:** Refuses to miss the night.

**story:**
Wynn is worn out, and still he refuses to go to bed. Every night he plants himself on his feet and fights to keep his heavy eyes open a little longer. The dark hours feel too quiet and too precious for him to sleep through them. He would sooner sway where he stands, half-dreaming, than miss a single moment of the stillness he loves. Wynn is a soulmate for everyone who stays up far too late, who knows they should rest but cannot let go of the stillness of a late, quiet night.

- **atmosphere:** Nocturnal · Hushed · Heavy-Eyed
- **personality:** Stubborn · Drowsy · Wistful
