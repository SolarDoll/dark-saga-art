# Aven

- **series (drop):** Nightsong
- **mood:** Comes alive when the sun goes down.

**story:**
Aven comes alive the moment the sun goes down. All day he saves his energy, and once night falls he is finally ready to go, with his playful little companion bouncing and tumbling on his back. The two of them treat the dark hours as their own private playground, with all the room in the world to move and create. While everyone else is winding down, Aven is only getting started. Aven is a soulmate for night people and late bloomers, for everyone who does their best living, thinking, and creating long after midnight.

- **atmosphere:** Nocturnal · Playful · Energized
- **personality:** Spirited · Playful · Easygoing
