# Senna

- **series (drop):** Wildbloom
- **mood:** Gives shape to drifting ideas.

**story:**
Senna spends his days half-asleep, catching the ideas that drift unseen through the air. Most of them arrive faint and unfinished, barely more than a feeling. He holds each one close as he dreams and helps it grow into something whole and beautiful. Senna is a soulmate for thinkers, writers, and creators, for anyone with a head full of half-formed ideas waiting to become real.

- **atmosphere:** Dreaming · Imaginative · Drifting
- **personality:** Imaginative · Dreamy · Quietly Inventive
