# Faelan

- **series (drop):** Wildbloom
- **mood:** His heart overflows with beauty.

**story:**
Faelan carries a heart so full it cannot stay hidden. Everything he feels rises up and spills out into the world as something lovely. He gives that beauty away freely and asks for nothing in return. Faelan is a soulmate for the warm-hearted and the generous, for anyone who loves to leave the world around them a little softer and brighter.
