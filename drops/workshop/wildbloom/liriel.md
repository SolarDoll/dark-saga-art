# Liriel

- **series (drop):** Wildbloom
- **mood:** Flowers bloom wherever he rests.

**story:**
Liriel brings life with him everywhere he goes. Wherever he settles, flowers wake up and begin to bloom across him. He cannot help it, growth follows him the way light follows the morning. He loves to watch small things open up and reach toward the sky. Liriel is a soulmate for creators, makers, and dreamers, for anyone who feels most alive when they are helping something grow.

- **atmosphere:** Blooming · Lively · Abundant
- **personality:** Nurturing · Joyful · Life-Bringing
