# Pell

- **series (drop):** Wildbloom
- **mood:** Hides beneath a single leaf.

**story:**
Pell likes to keep to himself, tucked away under one big leaf where the world cannot reach him. Crowds and noise wear him out, and he gathers his strength back in his own shade. Under his leaf he feels safe enough to be fully himself. He will share his little hideaway with the few people he truly trusts. Pell is a soulmate for introverts and homebodies, for anyone who needs a small, sheltered place to retreat and feel like themselves again.

- **atmosphere:** Sheltered · Quiet · Cozy
- **personality:** Shy · Gentle · Self-Contained
