# Sylin

- **series (drop):** Nightsong
- **mood:** Dances with the night moths.

**story:**
Sylin spends the night dancing in a ring of moths. They turn and drift around him in a soft, weightless circle. If your day was heavy, he pulls you into the dance until your shoulders feel light again. When you lose your way in the dark, he helps you find the nearest light and follow it home. Sylin is a soulmate for anyone worn out by a long day, who needs to feel light and a little bit found again.

- **atmosphere:** Dancing · Weightless · Lighthearted
- **personality:** Playful · Uplifting · Guiding
