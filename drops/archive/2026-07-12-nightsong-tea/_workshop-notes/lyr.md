# Lyr

- **series (drop):** Nightsong
- **mood:** Sings the night's songs with the wind.

**story:**
Lyr sings through the night together with the wind. The two of them trade songs about everything that happened during the day. They sing about the small joys, the things that went wrong, and the worries you carry to bed. By the time the song fades, those worries feel lighter and easier to set down. Lyr is a soulmate for anyone who needs to let the day go before they can rest, and who finds comfort in a gentle voice in the dark.

- **atmosphere:** Melodic · Windswept · Soothing
- **personality:** Expressive · Gentle · Reassuring
