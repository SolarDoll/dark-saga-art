# Fenn

- **series (drop):** Wildbloom
- **mood:** Keeps the patience of green places.

**story:**
Fenn lives at the pace of moss and fern. He has learned that good things take their time, and there is no need to hurry any of it. Sitting with him makes your breathing grow deeper and your shoulders come down. He finds contentment in damp mornings, green corners, and long, unbothered hours. Fenn is a soulmate for the patient and the grounded, for anyone who wants to step out of the rush and feel held by something steady.

- **atmosphere:** Restful · Green · Grounded
- **personality:** Patient · Steady · Peaceful
